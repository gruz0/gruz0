### [Say42](https://say42.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=readme)

**Write your week. Say42 posts it.**

A scheduler for people who write a lot on Threads and LinkedIn. Write the week in one sitting; Say42 posts it, one post at a time, every day.

- Whole threads on Threads, not just single posts
- Your own posting times per account, with one-click scheduling into them
- Drag a post to another day or hour on the calendar
- One post to Threads and LinkedIn, with its own text for each
- LinkedIn personal profile or company page
- Per-post analytics, a posting streak, and alerts when a scheduled post can't be sent

It also publishes to Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest.

### [Common Ground](https://gruz0.github.io/linkedin-relationship-navigator/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=readme)

**Find the right person in your LinkedIn network.**

A private, browser-only relationship workspace for a LinkedIn personal data export. It answers the questions the export itself doesn't:

- Which founders have I actually spoken with?
- Who did I contact without receiving a reply?
- Which connections have no matching conversation?

The ZIP archive is parsed in the browser and is never uploaded. No export yet? There is a fictional demo workspace to explore.
